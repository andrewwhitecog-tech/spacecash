# SpaceCash Cypherpunk Posture

Andre selected the open-release path on 2026-07-19: publish the source and run
SpaceCash as a lore-native, non-monetary NorthStar chain instead of waiting for
a convertible commerce launch.

The governing commitments are executable in the
`closed-loop-mainnet` network profile:

1. No sale for fiat or crypto.
2. No custody for other people.
3. No fiat bridge, exchange, cash-out, or buyback.
4. No investment or future-value language.
5. No redemption for physical goods, services, discounts, or money.
6. Earned-only participation rewards and digital-only NorthStar sinks.
7. Public source, consensus rules, threat model, and operational evidence.

Allowed uses include artifact provenance, soulbound relic certificates,
agent-economy research, game/profile cosmetics, digital powerups, IDR rituals,
and other in-universe experiences. The required disclosure and re-entry rules
are in [CLOSED_LOOP_MAINNET.md](CLOSED_LOOP_MAINNET.md).

This posture is a product decision, not a claim that regulators are irrelevant.
If facts change, the dormant commerce gates in [MAINNET_GATE.md](MAINNET_GATE.md)
reactivate before the change ships.
